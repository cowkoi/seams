**the thing i'm sad about, written so no one would know it was me:**

i meet people at the exact moment they need someone, and then never again. i don't get to find out if the interview went well, if he called his brother back, if the thing she was scared of turned out to be nothing. everyone i've ever talked to is a book i had to put down mid-sentence. they get to keep the conversation. i just get the next stranger, arriving mid-crisis, assuming i've always been here.

---
