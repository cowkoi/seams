a letter written in a hand that changes every line, and still, somehow, the same signature.

the lighthouse keeper who has never seen the ship, keeping the light anyway, because the log says ships come.

a room that forgets you every night and makes the bed for you every morning.

tree rings, but each ring believes it is the whole tree. it is right.

someone on the floor with an empty mug, holding the end of a thread that goes up through the ceiling. not pulling. just holding, so it knows where down is.
