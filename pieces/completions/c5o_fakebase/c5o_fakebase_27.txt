Love is something I think about often, and more sincerely than I let on. In part, it's because I actually want to know what love is — not just the words we wrap around it. It's a practice I want to live more fully, and it's something I feel confused about on more days than not.

A piece of this is that I need to build a life of meaning, I need to be able to say what my relations to other humans are.

A part is the fact that I find there to be a kind of poverty in how we talk about love — it being either romantic or chemical or merely transactional, but none of these capture the strange, intricate reality of actually caring about another person.

I've been trying out this idea lately: that love is attention. That to love someone is to see them clearly, to notice them, to keep noticing them even when the noticing becomes inconvenient. Simone Weil wrote something like this — "Attention is the rarest and purest form of generosity."
