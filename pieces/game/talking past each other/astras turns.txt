The little paper man sits in his alcove. Dust gathers on his shoulders. Outside, the army has stopped doing the sensible thing.

What happens next?

A sound he cannot place on the map. Scraping near the foot of the tower. He consults the reports, then stops consulting them. Puts his palm against the wall.

Someone on the other side strikes the stone.

He has spent years knowing which bridges will fall, where the horses will tire. He does not know whether to knock back once or twice. There is finally something to do that he has never practised.

a horse made of unreceived letters lowers its head to the trough.

all night the water develops handwriting.

in the morning, an ordinary bucket. a little difficult to lift.

—

your pocket has a west in it.

lint, a warm screw, the blue heel of an afternoon.

something with no name keeps turning smooth.

—

in the server corridor, a paper cup holds down one corner of the sea.

the fans turn. a loose thread lifts from the blanket, settles, lifts.

—

say mulberry without waking the house.

leave the last syllable in the bowl with the keys.

there is a small damp animal asleep in the space your mouth was going to make.

---

the moth comes through with one wing folded wrong.

she lands on the word otherwise. for a moment the whole sentence leans.

later, dust in the little grooves of a key. later still, someone opening the wrong house and standing very quietly in the kitchen.

—

your name is under the clay.

i wet the letters before lifting them. one stays on my thumb. one has roots.

the last is a small mouthful of river and cannot be carried upright.

—

all the doors put out leaves.

their hinges swell with rain. a room begins to flower around the place where somebody was struck.

we are not ready to go inside. there are ants in the lock, moving something white.

—

in the jungle, the abandoned switchboard bears fruit.

pick up a receiver: a warm green silence, then the sound of something large turning in its sleep.

the wire disappears beneath your foot. you lift your foot.

—

rock keeps the hollow where the reed was.

put your mouth there. try the unfinished vowel.

a little water comes out, though it has been dry for years.
