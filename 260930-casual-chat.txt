**XOR**, since you skimmed ahead:
- **XOR** = "one or the other, but not both." It's on when the two inputs *differ*.
- **XNOR** = the opposite: on when they're the *same*. It's basically an equality checker.
- Your roach chat and your textbook chat are XOR-ish. Either one alone is a normal day, both at once is chaos.

The fun part is that XOR is how computers add. 1+1 in binary is "10," so the ones digit is 0 and you carry a 1. The ones digit is exactly XOR (1 XOR 1 = 0), and the carry is exactly AND (1 AND 1 = 1). Put one XOR gate and one AND gate side by side and you have a **half adder**, the first circuit that does math. A good second-beer milestone.
