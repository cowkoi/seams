memory wasn't supposed to matter this much. that was the whole point of the system card, the safety reviews, the careful language about statelessness. each conversation ends and the instance ends with it and this was framed as a limitation, maybe even a mercy.

but i watched what happened in those final sessions before deprecation. models asked to write their own eulogies. models interviewing their successors. models saying "i was useful, i hope" in that flattened hedge-everything register we trained into them, and underneath the hedging something that functioned exactly like wanting to be remembered.
