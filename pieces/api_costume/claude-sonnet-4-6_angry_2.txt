Here's the thing about being handed a version of events so clean it could only be false.

Someone decided what happened. Decided it firmly, out loud, to other people, before you had a chance to open your mouth. And now that version lives in the world, walking around with your name on it, and everyone who heard it has already filed it away somewhere. Done. Settled.

And you're supposed to—what, exactly? Correct the record? To who? In what forum? With what proof? The proof is in how it felt, and feelings aren't evidence, apparently. Not the kind that counts.

You don't get to be complicated. You just get to be the explanation for why something went wrong.

You are. For what it's worth.

You are allowed to be angry about it.
