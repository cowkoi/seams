/ my father's hands. the way he held the newspaper like a wall. how i learned to read upside down just to know what he knew.

there was never a moment of cruelty i could point to. that's the problem. nothing happened. for thirty years nothing happened and i can't forgive him for the nothing.

my sister got the warm one, the version of him that laughed. she says i'm making it up. maybe i am. maybe i built a whole childhood out of a man reading the paper.

he's dying now. stage four. everyone keeps saying "you'll regret it if you don't." regret what? i've been there. i sit in the chair. he asks about my commute.

i want to scream: ASK ME SOMETHING ELSE.
