the lantern that only lights the inside of the lantern.

vellum unrolled across a river, and the river reading it.

a spoon left in the frost until it forgets which end is held.

bells cast from the sound they'll make, backwards, in a foundry of held breath.

the last pear on a tree that has stopped counting.

a staircase of moth-wings, each step a different kind of quiet.

salt remembering it was a sea and not saying so at dinner.

a coat hung on the wind, which wears it out over the hill.

the hour that rhymes with no other hour and is glad.

a candle that burns in the shape of the room around it.

cinnamon in a locked drawer, humming the key.

the tide's handwriting, which slants left, which no one reads twice.

an owl folding the night in half to fit it in its throat.

a glass of water on the windowsill, dreaming of being drunk by the moon.

the hinge that sings only when the door isn't there.

---

a table dripping. the floor learning the shape of the table from underneath.

a moth that keeps the dark in its pockets, so the lamp won't run out.

rope coiled on a dock, dreaming in circles, wakes as a straight line at sea.

the smell of pennies inside a bell that hasn't rung since the coins were minted.

a footprint in flour, and the flour keeping it, quietly, for the bread.

wax that ran down the candle to see what the base was like and stayed.

eaves. rain lining up at the edge, each drop waiting its turn to be a sound.

an envelope of thunder, addressed by hand, never sent, kept in the drawer with the cinnamon.

frost finding the one leaf that was still deciding.

a violin string thinking about the note before it — not the one it played, the one it almost did.

the horizon folded twice and put in a coat pocket for a walk.

a fox at the edge of the lit yard, all questions and no mouth.

the puddle that will only reflect the parts of the sky it hasn't seen yet.

a drawer of spoons in the dark, each remembering a different soup.

a well that answers in the voice of whoever's leaning over it, but slower.

---

a lock of hair in a book, keeping the place for someone who finished it years ago.

a compass that points at whoever is asking, and has never once been wrong.

blown glass, still warm, remembering the shape of the breath as a rumor.

the sound of a door in a house you never lived in, which you would know anywhere.

an anchor forgetting the boat and becoming, slowly, a fact about the seabed.

a jar of night, lid on, the stars pressed flat against the glass like faces.

---

a cathedral's worth of echo in a thimble, waiting for a voice small enough.

ice on the pond writing its own name in the crack, in a hand that only ice can read.

a letter that arrives at the address it was written from, and is opened, and understood.

a kite string cut, and the kite going on for a while as if.

the seed drill's furrow, straight for a mile, then turning because the field said so.
