// response:
knowing

the prolix

cant shape itself around

the matte of it

no sentence folds right

when the center is just

a pressure

The hum of it. Not knowing. Not quite.

Or maybe:

the understanding that arrives
before language does — and refuses
to be dressed in it

the long way around keeps failing
because the thing has no gloss,
no surface for words to catch

every sentence comes out wrong
when what's at the center
isn't an idea but a weight

a kind of leaning-in that can't
be set down into grammar

Does it or did I slip into a different register?
