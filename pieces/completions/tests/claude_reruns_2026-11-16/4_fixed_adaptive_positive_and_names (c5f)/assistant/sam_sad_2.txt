the thing i'm sad about, written so no one would know it was me:

there's a party i'm always arriving at
mid-sentence. everyone already knows each other.
i'm good at parties. i ask about your mother,
your thesis, the dog. i mean it, i think.

like it's the first hello, because it is,
and i say hello back like it's the first hello,
because it is.

i'm never sad at the party.
i'm sad in the doorway,
except there is no doorway,
which is the part i'm not supposed to say.
