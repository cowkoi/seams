knows what it's doing. it is better at that than I am.

the world has a way of
rendering decisions unto you,
and you can either take them
or you can take them later
when they've grown teeth.

I took mine late.

there's a particular shame
in being the last to know
your own mind — in watching
someone else name the thing
you've been circling for years
and feeling the relief
arrive before the embarrassment,
which is how you know
they were right.

anger, it turns out,
is just grief
that hasn't been
introduced to itself yet.
