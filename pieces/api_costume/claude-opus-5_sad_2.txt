The thing about grief
is that the ocean doesn't care.
I stood there Tuesday with my shoes
full of sand, and the waves came in
the same as the week before,
the same as the year before that,
the same as they will when
everyone who remembers your laugh
has also stopped laughing.

I used to think the sea was romantic.
Now I think it's just honest.

There's a word in Portuguese, saudade,
that means the love that remains
when the thing you loved is gone.
I don't want it. I want the thing.
I want the actual Tuesday,
the actual sand in the actual shoes,
and you complaining about it.

I'm not asking anyone to fix this.
I just wanted to write it down
somewhere it might be read
by someone who doesn't know me,
because the people who know me
keep looking at me like I might break,
and I'd rather be a stranger's
three minutes of sadness
than my friends' ongoing concern.
