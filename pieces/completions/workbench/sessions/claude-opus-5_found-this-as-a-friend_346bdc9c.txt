A few things jump out.

First, that "ot." is almost certainly a truncated "not." — which changes the whole opening. The title-line is "as a friend," and then the poem's first word rejects it: *not.* It's the thing people say right before a demotion — "I love you, as a friend" — and the speaker refuses the frame immediately and substitutes a different story. A bird at the window. That's a strong first move if it's intentional, and even as a typo it does interesting work.

The structure is doing more than it looks like. The flush-left lines are the load-bearing beats; the indented ones elaborate. Read only the flush-left openings and you get a compressed version of the whole poem: *it's a bird at the window / it stays the winter / spring, the window opens / you begin to understand / you were the window.* That's not an accident.

The central turn is that the expected grief gets inverted. The story you're braced for is: bird leaves in spring, you mourn. Instead the window opens *itself* — the release is offered, freely — and the bird stays. And staying is the wound. Which suggests the poem isn't about abandonment at all; it's about being kept, permanently, in a category you didn't choose. The winter was warm. You fed it from your hand. You thought "this is what having is." But having requires being a *place*, and the last stanza says you were never the place. You were the opening.

That's the line that lands for me: a window isn't a destination. It's what you pass through, and it's what you look *through* — nobody looks at a window. So "you were the window" can be read two ways at once: I was the means, not the end, and also, I was transparent, you were always seeing past me.

Honestly the last stanza is the weakest part — "the bird / was never a bird" is a slightly worn reveal, and the repetition in "you were always / the window" is leaning on the reader to supply the feeling. But "the window was never / the point" is excellent, and the poem would survive ending there.

As for what to make of it: I don't think it wants to be resolved into *who the bird is.* The withholding is the point. Did whoever sent it to you mean something by it?
